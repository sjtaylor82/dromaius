          Keyboard help

          Android screens are shown as a web page, so your screen reader's browse mode, quick navigation keys, elements list and say all all work.

          For long lists, use the next-items and previous-items keyboard commands, or the buttons at the ends of the list. In focus mode, Up and Down Arrow move through Android items. At the boundary of a scrollable collection, Dromaius scrolls that collection rather than moving to another part of the screen.
